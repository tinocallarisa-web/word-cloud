Quick Start

1. Add the visual to your report

In Power BI Desktop, go to the Visualizations pane → click the three dots → Get more visuals → search for Word Cloud by TCViz.

2. Choose your data

  • You already have words (keywords, product names, tags): drag that column to Words.
  • You have sentences (survey comments, ticket descriptions, reviews): drag that column to Text (free text). The visual splits it into words, drops filler words (“the”, “and”, “que”…) and counts the rest.

3. Optional: a measure in Size

Without it, each word is sized by how many times it appears. With it (sales, clicks, mentions), each word is sized by that measure.

4. Pick a look

Format → Style template gives you a finished design in one click (Clean, Bold, Editorial, Monochrome, Poster). Then choose a Shape and an Orientation. Anything you set by hand wins over the template.

Format on the canvas

In format mode, click the words to format them as text (font, size, colour) or click the shape or background to format those, without opening the Format pane.

Selection & filtering

  • Click a word to filter the rest of the page; Ctrl+click to add words; click the background to clear.
  • In Text mode a word stands for the rows that contain it, so clicking it filters to those rows (up to 200 rows per word).
  • Selections from other visuals highlight the words they touch and dim the rest.
  • Bookmarks restore the selected words.
  • Drilldown works with a hierarchy in Words (for example Category → Product). Free text has no hierarchy, so there is nothing to drill into: filter it instead.
  • Right-click a word for the Power BI context menu (Drill through, Include, Exclude…).

Accessibility

  • Keyboard: Tab into the visual, then ← → ↑ ↓ move from word to word in order of weight, Home/End jump to the first and last, Enter or Space selects (Ctrl to add), Esc clears, Shift+F10 opens the context menu.
  • Screen readers: each word is announced with its value.
  • High contrast: with a Windows high-contrast theme the words, shape and selection use the system colours.
  • Show as a table lists the words and values.
  • 18 colour-blind safe palettes.

Performance & limits

  • The layout engine checks collisions pixel by pixel on a bit-packed grid, so a cloud of 150 words usually draws in well under a second.
  • Rows per block: 30,000. In the Power BI service further blocks are requested automatically; in Power BI Desktop only the first block arrives (a Power BI limit for custom visuals) and the warning icon tells you.
  • Words drawn: up to Maximum words (1,000 at most). If some do not fit, the warning icon says how many.
  • Shape image: kept in memory once per image, not reloaded on every filter.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature on this page is included, with no licence, no trial period and no watermark.

Words or Text — which one do I use?

Words when each row already holds one word or phrase (a keyword, a product, a tag). Text when each row holds sentences people wrote (comments, answers, reviews) and you want the visual to find the words for you.

Why can I not drill down with free text?

Drilldown moves through the levels of a hierarchy. A column of comments has no levels: its words are worked out by the visual, not stored in the model. Use drilldown with a hierarchy in Words, and with free text click a word to filter the rows that contain it.

Which languages does free text understand?

Tokenizing works for any language that separates words with spaces or punctuation. Stopword lists are built in for English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian, and the visual detects which of them your text uses. For other languages, add the filler words to Exclude words.

How do I colour words by sentiment?

Put a sentiment score (a measure where negative means negative) in Color value and set Colors → Color by to Sentiment. Words lean red below zero and green above. With free text, each word takes the average score of the comments that mention it — so you see which words come with complaints and which with praise. A 1–10 score works too: make a measure that subtracts the midpoint (e.g. [Score] - 5.5).

Can I use my logo as the shape?

Yes. Store the image in the model as Base64 text (data:image/png;base64,…), put it in Shape image and set Layout → Shape to Image. Words fill the dark part of the image. A PNG with a transparent background works best.

Can the shape be a word?

Yes: Layout → Shape → Text, and type it in Shape text. The cloud fills the letters.

Why does the cloud look the same every time?

On purpose: the layout uses a fixed Seed, so your report does not reshuffle each time someone opens it. Change the seed for a different arrangement.

What are the small repeated words inside the shape?

Fill the shape adds smaller copies of your words to fill gaps, so the outline reads clearly. They are decoration only: tooltips, counts and the keyboard use the real words. Turn Fill the shape off to remove them.

Does it work with bookmarks?

Yes. Bookmarks keep the words you selected and show them selected when you return.

Which languages is the Format pane in?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean. It follows the language of Power BI.

Does my data leave Power BI?

No. The visual has no network permission: it cannot send or load anything. See Privacy.
