How the shape works
Words fill the inside of the shape, largest first, from the centre out.
The shape is cropped to its real outline, so the icon uses the whole visual.
Stretch shape to fill off keeps the proportions; on fills the visual.
Make it read at a glance
Show shape: a light silhouette behind the words (12% by default).
Shape border: an outline just outside the shape.
Fill the shape: small repeats close the gaps — decoration only, never counted.
Your own text or logo
Text: type a word in Shape text — the cloud fills the letters.
Image: a Base64 image in the Shape image field; words fill its dark area.
Web links are never loaded: the visual has no network access.
