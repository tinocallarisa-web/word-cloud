--- DESCRIPTION ---

The open answers in a survey are where people tell you why. A bar chart cannot show them, and nobody reads two thousand comments one by one.

Word Cloud by TCViz is free — every feature, no licence, no watermark. Drop in a column of comments: it detects the language, removes the filler words and counts the rest. Put the score in Color value and every word takes the average score of the comments that mention it — red words come with complaints, green ones with praise.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THIS VIDEO
• Survey comments in free text: stopwords removed, language detected
• Sentiment without DAX: the score as Average and a neutral value
• Shapes: an icon, your own text and the silhouette with an outline
• Style templates and palettes
• Click a word to filter the comments that contain it; bookmarks
• Formatting directly on the canvas

WHAT IT DOES
• Words or free text, with stopwords in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian
• Two-word phrases, exclusion list, minimum word length
• Rectangle, circle, ellipse, diamond, triangle, hexagon, 42 icons, your text or your logo
• 32 palettes (17 colour-blind safe), theme colours, fx, gradients and sentiment
• Cross-filtering, highlighting, bookmarks, drilldown, report page tooltips
• Keyboard navigation, screen reader labels, high contrast
• Format pane in 12 languages
• No network access: nothing leaves your report
