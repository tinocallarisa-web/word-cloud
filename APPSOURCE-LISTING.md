## Description

```
Most word clouds show you which words are big. They don't show you what people actually said, in which language, or whether they said it happily.

Word Cloud by TCViz is free — every feature, no licence, no watermark. Drop in a column of words, or raw survey comments: it detects the language, removes filler words and counts the rest. Shape the cloud as a circle, an icon, your own text or your logo; colour it by value, by sentiment or with 32 palettes, 17 of them colour-blind safe.

WORDS OR FREE TEXT

• Words: one word or phrase per row — keywords, products, tags — sized by a measure or by how often they appear.
• Text: comments, tickets, reviews. The visual splits them into words (accents, Cyrillic and CJK included), removes stopwords in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian, and detects which of those languages your text uses.
• Two-word phrases ("late delivery", "customer service"), minimum word length and an exclusion list.

SENTIMENT WITHOUT DAX

Drag a score to Color value, choose Average, set the neutral value (5.5 for a 1–10 score). Each word takes the average score of the comments that mention it: red words come with complaints, green ones with praise. The warning icon tells you if the score is being summed instead.

SHAPES

• Rectangle, circle, ellipse, diamond, triangle, hexagon and 42 icons
• Your own text — the words fill the letters
• An image from your model, such as your logo, stored as Base64
• A light silhouette, an outline and a dense fill so the shape reads at a glance

LOOK

• Style templates: Clean, Bold, Editorial, Monochrome, Poster
• 32 palettes, 17 colour-blind safe; theme colours; single colour with conditional formatting (fx); gradients by size or by a measure
• Horizontal, vertical, mixed or custom angles
• A fixed seed: the same report always draws the same cloud

POWER BI, ALL OF IT

• Click a word to filter the page; Ctrl+click for several. In Text mode a word filters the comments that contain it.
• Highlighting from other visuals, bookmarks, drilldown on a hierarchy, context menu
• Tooltips and report page tooltips
• Format on the canvas: click the words, the shape or the background to format them
• Keyboard navigation with screen reader labels, high contrast
• Format pane in 12 languages
• More than 30,000 rows in the Power BI service: the visual asks for the rest

PRIVACY

The visual has no network permission, so Power BI does not let it send or load anything. That is why your logo has to be stored in the model as Base64: a link to an image on a web server is not loaded, on purpose.

GETTING STARTED

1. Drag a column of words to Words — or comments to Text.
2. Optional: a measure to Size.
3. Format → Style template, then a Shape.

Documentation, FAQ and examples: https://tinocallarisa-web.github.io/word-cloud/support.html
Questions: https://github.com/tinocallarisa-web/word-cloud/discussions
Support: support@tcviz.com
```
